# Surface brief — adevwithpurpose.com home (v5 preview)

Scope: full home page. Mode: Persuade. Audience: founder/small-team lead; job: understand services, see tangible demos, email. Proof: public repos + per-service demo videos. Constraints: static no-build, indigo identity approved, mobile/reduced-motion readable, preview only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet showroom. One calm reading column where each of the four services is a split row - its words on one side, its client-update demo video on the other. It refuses the pinned-scene animation showcase and the card-grid clutter of v4; the page behaves like the developer: precise, calm, no noise.

OWN-WORLD: inherited indigo night - ground #070b18, panels as hairline-outlined dark glass used sparingly, one accent indigo #6366f1 doing real jobs (links, primary button, active states) with soft periwinkle #a5b4fc for secondary text accents. Space Grotesk display with tight tracking, Inter UI, JetBrains Mono reserved for numbers, labels and data. The glow lives only in the hero constellation; the rest of the page earns attention with space and type, not effects.

STORY: a founder reads one line of what he does, walks four services each anchored by a tangible demo video slot, checks proof they can click, learns how working together goes in three steps, and emails - the contact block is a single confident action with a copyable address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: header (brand, nav, availability pulse) over a dim constellation canvas; left-aligned hero - kicker "Safeer Ahmad · AI Developer", display headline "Software that does the busywork.", one-line sub naming the four services, chip row (availability, UTC+5, GitHub), primary "See the work", ghost email, YouTube link. Nothing else competes; the constellation is dimmed to ambience.

FORM: alternating split service rows - candidate 5 of the grounded list, the roll's lead (dealt 5/7/3), matching the brief's clean-and-easy-to-follow ask. seed: ae077f5e1958d39b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
